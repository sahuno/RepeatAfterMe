Repeat After Me

Visual lessons on repeated DNA in mouse and human. For anyone analysing repeats for the first time.

Colour key for a stretch of genome. Grey is not a repeat. Not to scale.

Focus on 4 main classes

LINE

Long. Has its own copying machinery.

SINE

Short. Uses the LINE machinery.

LTR

Retrovirus-like.

DNA

Cut and paste. Inactive in mouse and human.
