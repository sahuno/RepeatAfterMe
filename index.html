Mouse and human genomes

Repeat After Me

Visual lessons on the repeated DNA that fills a large share of every mammal's genome. Made for the newcomer who has to analyse repeats next month, and for anyone who is curious.

A stretch of genome, as this site colours it. Grey is not a repeat. Colours only; the strip is not to scale.

Four kinds of repeat, four colours, on every page

LINE

Long. Carries its own copying machine.

SINE

Short. Borrows the LINE machine.

LTR

Built like a retrovirus that stayed in.

DNA

Cut and paste. Long still, in mouse and human.
