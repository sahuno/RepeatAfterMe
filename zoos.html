Two zoos

Mouse and human carry different repeat families, and mouse has far more young copies, most of them ERVs that still cause new mutations. A mouse analysis that follows a human tutorial meets different names, more reads that fit many places, and copies that differ between strains.

Which ones still move

In the laboratory mouse, new insertions cause about 100 times the share of mutations they cause in people: about 0.1% of de novo mutations in humans (Maksakova et al. 2006). ERV insertions alone account for 10% to 12% of all spontaneous mouse mutations.

The 115 published mouse germline mutations caused by new insertions, from Gagnier et al. 2019; "other groups" is the remainder, including VL30 and ERV-β4.

  • L1: the mouse reference genome holds 2,811 structurally intact L1s and the human 146 (Gagnier et al. 2019). The original estimates were about 3,000 potentially active mouse L1s in three subfamilies, TF, A and GF (Goodier et al. 2001), and 80 to 100 retrotransposition-competent L1s in an average person (Brouha et al. 2003).
  • Human: "Human retrotransposons are LINE-1, Alu, and SVA" (Kazazian and Moran 2017); Alu and SVA move with LINE-1's proteins. "Unlike in inbred mice, infectious human ERVs have not been described, no new insertions have been found" (Maksakova et al. 2006).

What this means for your analysis

  1. Expect different names. The mouse table has no SVA, no HERV-K and no HERV-H; its only rows named HERV are 630 copies of four ERVL subfamilies such as HERV16-int and HERVL40-int. Its ERVs that still move are IAP, ETn, MLV and their relatives. Mouse B1 is filed in family Alu (Three names explains why), and mouse B2 and B4 have no human label at all.
  2. Expect more multi-mapping. Young copies are nearly identical, so their reads fit many places (One read, many places). In the simulated-read test, the 16 young mouse subfamilies kept 50.4% of their reads as unique, the 16 young human ones 65.5% (Counting tools).
  3. Expect your strain to differ from the reference. "Like mouse L1s and ERVs, these mouse SINEs are insertionally polymorphic in inbred strains" (Gagnier et al. 2019), and 84% of the IAP mutations with a known strain arose in C3H mice. Copies in your mice can be missing from the C57BL/6 reference, and reference copies can be missing from your mice.
  4. Do not read a human tutorial's list of active elements as a mouse list. In mouse, the ERVs are the main source of new insertions; in human, they cause none.
